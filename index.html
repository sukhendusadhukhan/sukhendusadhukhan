Dr. Sukhendu Sadhukhan

Dr. Sukhendu Sadhukhan is a physicist and researcher currently working as a Post-Doctoral Researcher at SATIE, ENS Paris-Saclay, University Paris-Saclay, France. His work spans condensed matter physics, multiferroics, nanomaterials, functional materials, device fabrication, instrumentation automation, scientific data acquisition, and interdisciplinary academic research. He has authored 27 published research articles, holds a published patent, and brings strong experience in teaching, student mentorship, and research laboratory development.
